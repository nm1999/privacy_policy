Communication Information: If you contact us, we may keep a record of that correspondence.

Waste Details: Information you provide about the type and quantity of waste for pickup (e.g., "household waste," "recyclables," "hazardous waste" if applicable).

Location Information (with your permission): We may collect precise or approximate location information from your mobile device if you grant us permission to do so. This is essential for our waste pickup services to locate your pickup address and for our drivers to efficiently plan routes. You can enable or disable location services when you use our Service at any time, through your device settings.
